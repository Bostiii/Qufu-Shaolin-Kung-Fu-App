# 🥋 Kung Fu School App

A mobile app for the Qufu Shaolin Kung Fu School teaching Tai Chi, Shaolin Kung Fu, Wing Chun and Sanda. It helps new students get started, keeps current students up to date, and gives everyone a single place for the school's schedule, news and information.

> **Status:** 🚧 In planning / early development

---

## ✨ Features

### Onboarding & Accounts
- Introduction screen
- Registration / Login
- Language selection (English and Chinese first, more languages later)
- Old student / new student selection
- Tutorial for new students

### Main Content
- Main page
- School rules
- Separate app sections:
  - Tai Chi overview
  - Shaolin Kung Fu overview
  - Wing Chun overview
  - Sanda overview
  - Staff overview
  - Masters overview
  - School overview
  - Things to do outside of training
- New student checklist (what to bring and prepare)
- How to get to the school

### Media
- Built-in videos and photos
- **Fully offline compatible** – no external links, all media is bundled in the app

### Live Information
- Live schedule tab
- Notifications
- News for upcoming events
- Weekly cleaning duty tab (who has to clean this week)
- Masters on duty tab

### General
- Settings
- Compatible with both Android and iOS
